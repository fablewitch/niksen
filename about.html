About

A field guide to keeping less and noticing more.

Niksen is a quiet, unhurried journal about minimalism, herbalism, and the practice of being present, one small experiment at a time.

niksen is Dutch for doing nothing, on purpose.

the windowsill, late afternoon

I work in tech, where for years my job was more or less to add things, features and meetings and notifications, and I was good at it. The work moved at a speed I never quite chose, though, and somewhere in the hum of it I stopped noticing my own days.

The turn came the winter I could no longer say what I'd done with my week, so I started subtracting, first the possessions and then the commitments, and after those the low half-attention running underneath everything else. What grew back in the space was small and green, a windowsill of herbs and a slower relationship with time. Some of that has felt like healing, though it's the quiet sort that's hard to point at.

This blog is where I keep the notes on what I've let go of, what I've planted, and what paying attention has taught me about which things are worth keeping. Nothing here is prescriptive, and there's no name on it on purpose. It's one person's field guide to a lighter, calmer life, written down in case any of it is useful to yours.

What you'll find here

Minimalism Notes on owning less, what I've let go of and what stayed, and the quiet arithmetic of enough.
Herbalism A beginner's windowsill, where I grow, dry and brew simple herbs, one forgiving plant at a time.
Living in the moment Small experiments in attention, like leaving the phone in another room, or doing one thing at a time.

A letter, now and then

No schedule and no noise, just an occasional note when there's something worth slowing down for.
