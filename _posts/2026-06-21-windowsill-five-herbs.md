If you have killed a plant — and who hasn't — start here. These five ask for little and forgive much.

## The forgiving five

Mint, thyme, chamomile, lemon balm, and calendula will all tolerate a missed watering and a less-than-perfect sill. Begin with one, learn its moods, then add the next.
