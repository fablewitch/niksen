For one year I made a single rule: nothing new came into the house unless something left to make room for it. A book for a book. A jumper for a jumper.

The emptier shelves were the least of it. What changed was the pause before every purchase — the small friction of asking what I'd be willing to give up in exchange.

## The pause did the work

Most things never made it past that question. The rule wasn't really about subtraction; it was about attention at the threshold.
