Most of us have killed a plant at some point, so this is the list I would hand to anyone starting again. These five ask for little and forgive a lot.

## The forgiving five

Mint, thyme, chamomile, lemon balm, and calendula will all tolerate a missed watering and a less-than-perfect sill. Start with just one and get to know how it behaves before you add the next.
