Small, woody, and hardy through the kind of neglect that kills softer herbs.

## Growing it

Give it sun and gritty, well-drained soil, then mostly leave it alone. Overwatering is the only real risk.

## Brewing a cup

A sprig or two steeped for a few minutes makes a warming, savoury tea, good with honey when a cold is circling.
