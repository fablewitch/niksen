Bright, lemony, and almost impossible to discourage. Lemon balm spreads eagerly, so many keep it in a pot to save the garden bed.

## Growing it

Happy in sun or part shade and forgiving of most soils. Pinch it back often to keep it bushy and stop it flowering too soon.

## Brewing a cup

A small handful of fresh leaves, torn, over hot water for a few minutes. Bright enough to drink through the day, gentle enough for the evening.
