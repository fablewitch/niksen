Loves sun and dry, poor soil, and the harder its life the more fragrant the buds.

## Growing it

Plant in the sunniest, best-drained spot you have. Prune after flowering to stop the stems hardening and going bare at the base.

## Brewing a cup

Use buds sparingly, since a small pinch is plenty and too much turns the cup soapy. Lovely blended with chamomile before bed.
