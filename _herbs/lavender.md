Loves sun and dry, poor soil — the harder its life, the more fragrant the buds.

## Growing it

Plant in the sunniest, best-drained spot you have. Prune after flowering to keep it from going woody.

## Brewing a cup

Use buds sparingly — a small pinch is plenty, as too much turns soapy. Lovely blended with chamomile before bed.
