Cheerful orange flowers that dry beautifully and self-seed happily around the garden.

## Growing it

Easy from seed in a sunny spot. Deadhead often and it will bloom until the frosts.

## Brewing a cup

Dried petals make a golden, mild tea; the same infusion, cooled, is a gentle rinse for tired skin.
