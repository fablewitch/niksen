For one year I made a single rule: nothing new came into the house unless something left to make room for it, so a new book had to be paid for with an old one, and a jumper with a jumper.

What changed most was the pause before every purchase, the small friction of asking what I would be willing to give up in exchange. The emptier shelves mattered far less than that.

## The pause did the work

Most things never made it past that question. The rule turned out to be about attention at the threshold more than about subtraction.
