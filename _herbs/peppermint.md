Nearly impossible to kill, and just as hard to contain — grow it in a pot unless you want it everywhere.

## Growing it

It likes damp soil and doesn't mind some shade. Cut it hard and it comes back thicker.

## Brewing a cup

Fresh leaves, lightly crushed, over hot water. A cup after dinner settles things nicely.
